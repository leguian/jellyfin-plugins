Genre thumbnails

Thumbnails used by the "All genres" section when its cards are set to "Custom images" (format button of the section, in the editor). One image per card shape: the section picks the one matching its shape, else another uploaded one (cropped), else the collage of four posters.

PNG, JPEG or WebP, 5 MB maximum. Larger images are fine, smaller ones look blurry on high density screens.
